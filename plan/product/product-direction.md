# Product Direction

AgentMS is an agent-centered hostel listing and room availability management system for Ghana's student accommodation market.

The previous product was manager-centered: hostel managers owned hostel records, managed tenants, took payments, tracked reservations, received reports, and interacted with the marketplace/admin systems. The pivot changes the primary operator from hostel manager to trusted accommodation agent.

In the Ghana hostel workflow, agents are the people students already rely on. Agents gather room information from hostel managers, porters, caretakers, or property owners, then keep students updated when rooms are available. AgentMS should make that workflow structured, searchable, trustworthy, and easier to maintain.

The first product goal is not a full property management suite. The first goal is:

- Agents can register and manage their profile.
- Agents can create, read, update, delete, publish, unpublish, and refresh room listings.
- Students can discover rooms and contact agents.
- Admins can verify agents, moderate listings, and monitor activity.
- The system can track availability freshness so students know whether a listing is current.
