# Stable Decisions

- AgentMS is agent-first.
- Managers are not the MVP user target.
- Listings are agent-owned.
- Property records can be shared across agents later.
- Availability freshness is a first-class feature.
- Marketplace is deferred.
- Payments are excluded from the platform.
- Student portal history, saved preferences, and remembered activity require an authenticated student account.
- Old auth, payment, email, and Firebase keys must not be reused.
- Waterfall artifacts must be completed and reviewed before broad implementation.
